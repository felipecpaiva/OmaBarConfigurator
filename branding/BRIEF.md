# OmaBar brand brief

The brief behind every logo variation. Written 2026-11-16, before any image was made, so the variations can be
judged against it and not against taste alone. Same shape as the omagnet brief.

## What the project is, in one line

A panel for the Omarchy bar: show, hide and reorder its widgets, draw rounded pills behind groups of them, and save
layouts to come back to.

## Who it is for

Omarchy users who tune their bar. Terminal-comfortable people who like a tidy, deliberate desktop, read a README
before they install, and judge a project by its logo and its first screenshot in a few seconds.

## What the brand has to say

1. **Order on the bar.** Loose icons become neat groups. Pills, rhythm, a calm strip.
2. **At home on Omarchy.** It looks like it belongs beside Omarchy's own apps and themes, without pretending to be
   official.
3. **Small and precise.** A sharp utility, not a platform. Sibling of omagnet and omahop by the same maker.

## Name

`OmaBar`, one word, capital O and B. The `Oma` is the community's Omarchy prefix (omagnet, omahop, Omaland);
`Bar` is the thing it edits. Say it "oh-ma-bar". The plugin id stays
`io.github.felipecpaiva.omabarconfigurator`.

## The idea to draw from

The product's own look: a thin horizontal bar with rounded pills sitting on it, each pill holding a few dots or
glyphs, with a clear gap between pills. Splitting and joining pills is the core action (the Groups page draws a
mini bar with seams you click).

## References, and what not to take from them

| Reference | What to learn from it | What must NOT be copied |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| **Omarchy** (`/usr/share/omarchy/logo.svg`, `icon.png`) | Chunky pixel blocks, a single bright colour on a plain ground, monospace. | Its logo or icon, or anything that reads as an official Omarchy mark. Omarchy has not endorsed this project. |
| **Waybar, macOS menu bar tools (Bartender, Ice)** | The idea of a tidy menu bar. | Their icons, names or styling. |
| **omagnet** (`../omagnet/branding/`, same maker) | Family feel: graphite ground, one accent, JetBrains Mono, works at 16 px. | Its exact mark (Snap Grid). OmaBar needs its own silhouette. |

Names appear only as plain text, with the disclaimer in the README: not affiliated with or endorsed by Omarchy.

## Constraints every variation must meet

- **Works small.** Readable at 16 and 32 pixels, in one colour.
- **Works on light and dark.** A one-colour version for each (Omarchy themes "White" and "Tokyo Night").
- **No gradients needed.** The mark stands without gradient, shadow or glow.
- **Vector first.** A real SVG source for the icon and the wordmark; PNG exports come from it.
- **Own idea.** Different from Omarchy's and omagnet's marks at a glance, even as a thumbnail.
- **Text rules.** No em dash or en dash anywhere; plain English.
- **Licence.** Made for this project; released under the project's MIT licence unless the maintainer chooses otherwise.

## Deliverables for the chosen variation

1. Icon: `icon.svg`, PNG at 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 32, 16; one-colour light and dark; a monochrome mask.
2. Wordmark and horizontal lockup, on light, dark and transparent.
3. GitHub social preview, 1280 by 640, under 1 MB.
4. README banner with the tagline.
5. Marketplace `preview.png` at the repo root.
6. Terminal banner in plain text (half-block letters).
7. Demo GIFs per feature, screenshots of the panel.
8. One-page brand sheet: palette with contrast ratios, type, spacing, do and do not.
