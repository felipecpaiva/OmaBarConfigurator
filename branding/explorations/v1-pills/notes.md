# Pill rhythm

Two rounded groups of different lengths sit above a short rail. The mint right group is the selected group. Clear one-pixel gaps preserve each group at 16 px.

Palette: graphite #16181d; near white #f4f5f7; accent #5cdbc0. No gradients, shadows or glow.

Typeface: JetBrains Mono Bold, outlined from the installed font into real SVG paths. The masters have no font dependency.

A low horizontal rhythm, not Omarchy's chunky pixel lettering or notched frame. Unlike omagnet's Snap Grid, it has no square ring, four quarters or snapping corner.

Small use: the master uses a 16 by 16 coordinate grid. Review icon-16.png at native size. The seam and gaps remain open without colour.

Dark surfaces: icon.svg, wordmark.svg and lockup.svg. Light surfaces: icon-light.svg, wordmark-light.svg and lockup-light.svg. One colour: icon-mono.svg is graphite; icon-mono-dark.svg is near white. All icon backgrounds are transparent.

concept.png is a 1024 px vector-rendered sheet. A generated codex-concept.png was omitted because the image tool saves outside branding/, contrary to the task's write boundary.
