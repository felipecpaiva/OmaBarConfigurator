OmaBar shows, hides, reorders and groups the widgets on the Omarchy bar. The mark: two rounded groups of different lengths above a rail, the right one selected in mint. Order on the bar, small and calm.

1. The mark

App icon on a graphite tile: icon.svg, icon-*.png
Glyph on dark: glyph.svg
Glyph on light, deep mint: glyph-light.svg
One colour: stencil.svg, stencil-white.svg
64, 32, 16 px. At 48 px and below use icon-small.svg (mark on its native pixel grid)

Text uses Near white, Graphite and the two Muted greys (AA or better). Mint and Mint deep are pill colours. The approved mint fails on light grounds (Mint #5cdbc0 on #f4f3f0 is 1.53:1; Mint #5cdbc0 on #ffffff is 1.70:1), so light files use Mint deep. No gradients, shadows or glows in the mark.

3. Type

JetBrains Mono

Bold for headings and the wordmark, regular for body. It is Omarchy's default font (SIL OFL licence), so OmaBar looks at home on the desktop it targets. Fallback: any monospace.

The wordmark OmaBar is JetBrains Mono Bold converted to outlines, one colour. One word, capital O and B. Say it "oh-ma-bar".

4. Spacing and shape

  • The mark lives on a 16 unit grid: pills 4 high with fully round ends, 1 unit gaps, a rail 1 high.
  • Base unit 8 px. Card padding 8% of the width, gaps 16 or 24 px.
  • Clear space around the mark: one pill height (4 grid units, a quarter of the icon) on every side.
  • Minimum size: 16 px for the icon (icon-small), 120 px wide for the lockup.
  • Rounded everywhere, like the pills: icon tile radius 22% of its width, pills fully round.

5. Do and do not

  • Use the graphite tile icon for app, favicon and avatar slots, the glyph on flat grounds.
  • Use the light files (*-light.*, Mint deep) on light pages.
  • Write the name as OmaBar: one word, capital O and B.
  • Keep the not-affiliated line near any mention of Omarchy.
  • Keep text short and plain.
  • Recolour, stretch or rotate the mark, or add a glow, shadow or gradient.
  • Put the light mint on a light ground, or set body text in either mint.
  • Use Omarchy's logo, or any styling that reads as an official Omarchy mark.
  • Write Omabar, OMABAR or Oma Bar.
  • Use an em dash or en dash in any text.
